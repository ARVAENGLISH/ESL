Practice

1 · Past tense

Use was/were with born: I was born here. (NOT: I born here.)
Don't use be with die: He died last year. (NOT: He was died last year.)

Complete the conversation.

2 · Used to

Use the base form in questions and negatives: Did you use to play sports? I didn't use to like bananas. (NOT: didn't used to)
Don't use never with didn't: I never used to wear sunglasses.

Complete the conversations with the correct form of used to.

Speaking

Pair work: add three questions, then take turns asking and answering. Ask follow-up questions!

Class activity: tell the class two interesting things about your partner.
