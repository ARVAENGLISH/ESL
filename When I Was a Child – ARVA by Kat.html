    2Discovery

    Think first, then click to check! 🔍

    1. What does "USE TO" mean?
    It talks about something you did regularly (a habit) or a situation that was true in the past, but not anymore. It's a finished past.
    2. How do you say in Spanish: "When I was a child I used to watch telenovelas all the time"?
    «Cuando era niña solía ver telenovelas todo el tiempo» (también: «acostumbraba a ver…»). In Spanish we use the imperfect: veía.
    3. When is it USED TO and when is it USE TO?
    USED to → affirmative: I used to play.
    USE to → questions and negatives, because did / didn't already shows the past: Did you use to play? I didn't use to play.

    3Grammar

    Used to

    Used to refers to something that you regularly did in the past but don't do anymore.

    Did you use to collect things?

    – Yes, I used to collect comic books.

    – No, I didn't use to collect anything, but now I collect old records.

    What sports did you use to play?

    – I used to play baseball and volleyball.

    – I never used to play sports, but now I play tennis.
