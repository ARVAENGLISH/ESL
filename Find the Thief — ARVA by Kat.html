🚨 EVIDENCE

LOOK — Why description matters

A crime happens. The police need to find the suspect, but they don't know who it is. A good description can help a lot!

🔎 It helps the police identify suspects.
👮 It helps witnesses say what they saw.
🎨 It helps forensic artists (police sketch artists) draw a sketch of the face.
🕵️ It helps investigators see the difference between one person and another.

Use specific details. Don't be vague.

❌ He is a normal man.

✅ He is tall with short, dark hair and a scar next to his mouth.

👮 How to describe a suspect

1. Start with the general: gender, age, height.
2. Then describe hair, eyes and face.
3. Add clothes and accessories.
4. Say the special features: a scar, a beauty mark, a piercing.
5. Put the adjectives in the correct order.

How tall is he?

How long is her hair?
She has short, wavy, blond hair.
📝 DESCRIPTION

Key rule — order for descriptions

Adjectives that describe a noun usually follow this order:

beautiful → long → straight → young → blond
She has beautiful, long, straight, blond hair.
