🛫 Grammar explanation · 8'

1. Verb TO BE

SubjectPresentPast
I / He / She / Itam / iswas
You / We / Theyarewere
FormStructureExample
➕ PositiveSubject + was/wereI was in Cartagena.
➖ NegativeSubject + wasn't/weren'tShe wasn't there. They weren't tired.
❓ QuestionWas/Were + subject?Were you at the beach? Yes, I was. / No, I wasn't.
❓ Wh- questionWh- + was/were + subject?Where were you last night?

2. Pronunciation focus: WHERE vs WERE 🎧

WHERE /wɛr/ = ¿dónde? → strong, open sound (like "wear").
WERE /wɜːr/ (strong) or /wər/ (short, in a sentence) = past of are.
In Where were you last night? the word where is strong and were is short and weak: /wɛr wər ju/.
