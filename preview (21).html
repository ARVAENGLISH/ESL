THE LONELINESS
EPIDEMIC

Are we more connected than ever… but lonelier than ever?

🎯 CLASS GOAL

By the end of the class, students will be able to discuss loneliness and social connection while choosing and using the appropriate past and perfect tenses to talk about past events, experiences, changes, and hypothetical situations.

🔥 WARM UP

1. Do you think people are lonelier today than they were in the past? Why?
2. What's the difference between being alone and feeling lonely?
3. Can social media make people feel more connected and more lonely at the same time?
4. Why do you think it can be difficult for adults to make new friends?
5. When do you personally feel most connected to other people?

💡 SOLITUDE ≠ ISOLATION

Solitude → being alone can be positive or intentional.

Isolation → being separated from others, often unwanted or harmful.

🔎 LANGUAGE DISCOVERY

PAST SIMPLE vs. PRESENT PERFECT vs. PAST PERFECT

¿Cuándo lo usamos? Formula Person + Example
Past Simple
Para hablar de una acción terminada en el pasado, especialmente cuando sabemos cuándo ocurrió. 		Person + verb in past + complement Many people moved to different cities during the pandemic.
Present Perfect
Para hablar de experiencias o situaciones que comenzaron en el pasado pero tienen conexión con el presente, o cuando no decimos cuándo ocurrió. 		Person + have/has + past participle + complement Many adults have experienced loneliness at some point in their lives.
Past Perfect
Para hablar de una acción que ocurrió antes de otra acción en el pasado. 		Person + had + past participle + complement She had lost contact with most of her friends before she realized she was lonely.
If + Past Perfect
Para imaginar una situación diferente en el pasado y hablar de una consecuencia que no ocurrió. 		If + person + had + past participle, person + would have + past participle If she hadn't moved away, she would have stayed closer to her friends.

🧠 REMEMBER

What is my reference point?

🕐 Past Simple → a finished moment in the past

🔗 Present Perfect → past + connection to now

⏪ Past Perfect → before another past event

💭 If + Past Perfect → an unreal alternative in the past

🗣️ PRODUCTION

Are We Really More Connected?

1. Do you think people have become lonelier in recent years? Why or why not?
2. Have you ever felt lonely even though you were surrounded by people? What had happened before you felt that way?
3. Have your friendships changed as you've gotten older? How?
4. Do you think social media has improved or damaged our relationships? What have you noticed?
5. Think about a time when you drifted apart from someone. What had happened before the relationship changed?
6. If social media hadn't become so popular, how do you think people's relationships would be different today?
🎯 Try to naturally use:

🟠 Past Simple — a specific past event

🔵 Present Perfect — experiences or situations connected to now

🟣 Past Perfect — something that happened before another past event

🔴 If + Past Perfect — an alternative past situation

🎭 CHALLENGE

THE LONELINESS DEBATE

Situation:

A community center is planning a new program to help adults build stronger social connections.

Two people have been invited to discuss why adults are becoming more isolated and what can be done about it.

👤 STUDENT A — THE COMMUNITY ORGANIZER

You organize activities for adults in your city.

You believe loneliness has increased because people:

  • work from home
  • spend too much time online
  • have moved away from family and friends
  • don't make enough time for meaningful relationships
Your goal:

Convince Student B that the community needs more opportunities for people to connect in person.

Ask questions, give examples, and explain what has changed.

👤 STUDENT B — THE REMOTE WORKER

You work from home and don't completely agree with Student A.

You believe people aren't necessarily lonelier. You think technology has helped people:

  • maintain long-distance friendships
  • meet people with similar interests
  • communicate more easily
  • have more control over their social lives
Your goal:

Challenge Student A's ideas and explain your own experience.

🗣️ DURING THE CONVERSATION

☐ Disagree with each other at least twice

☐ Ask each other follow-up questions

☐ Give at least one personal example

☐ Reach a conclusion together

Final question:

What is one realistic thing communities could do to help people feel less lonely?

🎯 LANGUAGE CHALLENGE

Don't force the grammar. Focus on communicating, reacting, disagreeing, and keeping the conversation going.
