Culture

In North America, people often sell old things like furniture, jewelry, or clothing at a garage sale or yard sale. They decide on prices, put the things on tables in their garage or yard, and then they put a sign in front of their house to advertise the sale. People come to look and maybe to buy. Some things at yard sales are antiques and are worth a lot of money.
