Hi, my name is Kat. I’m an English and Spanish teacher. I’m 29 years old, and I live in Cali.

My day starts early in the morning at 4:20 a.m., when I wake up. Then, I get dress and drive to the gym, I work out until around 6:10 a.m. Once I go back home I always eat the same thing: three eggs, one arepa, and a cup of black coffee, no sugar

On Mondays and Thursdays, I teach Spanish in the morning, but on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, I don’t teach Spanish; I only teach English.

In the afternoon, I take a break from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. every day. I like to eat a snack after lunch usually something sweet like yogurt and fruit, and sometimes I take a nap.

I have two classes at night, and I love my classes and my students. I finish my day at 8:00 p.m. After my last class, I have dinner and walk my dog. I usually go to bed early, around 9:30 p.m. I like to take a shower before bed.
