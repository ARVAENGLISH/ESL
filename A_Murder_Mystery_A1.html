🐍 THE REBELS

HE / SHE / IT

Desde entonces tienen un trauma: SIEMPRE LE PONEN “S” AL FINAL A TODO.
🟠 The neighbor listens to a discussion.

🟠 He lives nearby.

🔵 THE SNAKE TRAUMA

Pero el veneno de la serpiente les dejó otro trauma.
Ahora, para poder preguntar necesitan: DO / DOES

I / YOU / WE / THEY → DO

🔵 Do they live here?
🔵 Do you know the victim?
🔵 Do they watch TV?

HE / SHE / IT → DOES

🔵 Does the man work from home?
🔵 Does she know the victim?
🔵 Does he live here?

🚨 THE GOLDEN RULE

IF DOES IS THERE, THE VERB DOES NOT CHANGE.

❌ Does the man works from home?

✅ Does the man work from home?

DOES already carries the S.

🚨🚨 THE GOLDEN RULE OF THE CASE

WE CAN'T USE TO BE + ANOTHER VERB TOGETHER.

❌ He is has a knife on his hand.

Esto es imposible. Es como decir: “Él está tiene un cuchillo en la mano.”
Solo puedes usar un verbo a la vez. Elige sabiamente, detective.

🟢 He is dead.   OR   🟠 He has a knife.

But never: ❌ He is has a knife.

🚨 OKAY, DON'T PANIC.

TO BE y PRESENT SIMPLE NO SON LA MISMA COSA.
TO BE solamente sirve para decir SER o ESTAR.
I am a witness. → Yo SOY un testigo.
He is dead. → Él ESTÁ muerto.
She is at home. → Ella ESTÁ en casa.
Si quieres hablar de una acción, necesitas otro verbo:
work, live, watch, kill, have, go, know, need...
She works.
He watches TV.
They live here.

👥 I / YOU / WE / THEY

El verbo NO cambia.
I work.   You work.   We work.   They work.

🐍 HE / SHE / IT

Normalmente se le agrega S.
He works.   She lives.   It works.

🔵 QUESTIONS

DO: I / you / we / they
DOES: he / she / it
Do they live here?
Does she work here?
Does he know the victim?
IMPORTANTE: Cuando aparece DOES, el verbo vuelve a su forma básica.
❌ Does she works?   ✅ Does she work?

🚨 THE GOLDEN RULE

❌ He is has a knife.
Esto es incorrecto. Es como decir: “Él está tiene un cuchillo.”
Elige:
🟢 He is dead.
🟠 He has a knife.

Pero no: ❌ He is has a knife.
