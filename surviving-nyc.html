Step 5

Grammar Core: The Big Apple Problems

Lee cada regla con los ejemplos de Nueva York. Pregunta siempre: ¿lo puedo contar? Si sí, es contable; si no, es incontable.

1. Overload

Mucho con demasiado

too much + uncountable
  • There is too much noise and too much traffic in Times Square.
  • Rent costs too much money.
too many + countable
  • There are too many people in the subway.
  • There are too many rats in the streets.

2. Sufficiency

Lo necesario / suficiente

enough + countable / uncountable
  • Do you have enough space in a tiny Manhattan apartment?
  • There aren't enough quiet parks on busy days.

3. Comparison

Más y menos

more + both
  • NYC has more opportunities, but it costs more money.
fewer + countable
  • I wish there were fewer subway delays. (lo puedo contar)
less + uncountable
  • You have less space in NYC than in other cities. (no lo puedo contar)

4. Questions & Negatives

How many → cuántos / cuántas · How much → cuánto / cuánta

How many + countable
  • How many billboards can you see in Times Square?
not much + uncountable
  • There isn't much time to relax in New York.

Countable ✋ (lo puedo contar)

many · too many · fewer · how many · more · enough

Uncountable 🌫️ (no lo puedo contar)

much · too much · less · how much · more · enough

Should I move to New York City?

Elige tu rol. Tu profe hace el otro. Creen juntos un diálogo de 2 minutos. Debes usar por lo menos 4 cuantificadores: márcalos abajo cada vez que los uses.

Student A

The Dreamer ✨

Loves NYC. Wants to move there for job opportunities, energy and fun.

Student B

The Realist 🧐

Lives in NYC or knows the reality. Warns about cost, noise, small apartments and crowds.

Example starter

Student A · DreamerI want to move to NYC! There are more opportunities and many fun places to visit.
