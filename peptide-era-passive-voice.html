Passive voice · Time check

What is the passive voice?

We use the passive voice when the focus is on what happens to a person or thing, not on who does the action.

Active: Researchers studied the treatment. → Focus: WHO? Researchers
Passive: The treatment was studied. → Focus: WHAT HAPPENED? It was studied.

What about BY? by + person/organization tells us who did the action: The treatment was studied by researchers. But by is not always necessary — we omit it when the person is unknown, obvious, or not important.

TenseFormulaExample
Present simpleam/is/are + V3Peptides are studied.
Past simplewas/were + V3Peptides were studied.
Present perfecthas/have + been + V3Peptides have been studied.
Present continuousam/is/are + being + V3Peptides are being studied.

KEY: Passive = BE + V3

Roleplay — doctor & patient

A patient wants to lose weight at any cost and has come to the doctor asking about peptide treatments. The doctor needs to ask questions, explain the risks, and discuss safer, evidence-based options.
🩺 Doctor
  • What have you been taking so far?
  • Where was it bought? Who was it made by?
  • Has it been prescribed by a doctor?
  • Explain: the dose and purity may not be guaranteed.
  • Suggest evidence-based options under medical supervision.
🙋 Patient
  • I've seen peptides being promoted on social media.
  • I want results fast — I'll do anything.
  • Is it safe to inject them?
  • What options are recommended for me?

Use at least 5 passive sentences. Then switch roles.
