4 · GRAMMAR CHECK

Simple Past ✅ Positive

Regular verbs → +ED
RuleBase → Past
Most verbs: + edplay → played, kiss → kissed
Ends in e: + dlove → loved, decide → decided
Consonant + y → iedstudy → studied, cry → cried
Short verb (CVC): double the last letterstop → stopped, plan → planned
Top 10 irregular verbs (memorize them!)
go → wenthave → haddo → didsee → sawget → got make → madetake → tooksay → saidcome → cameeat → ate

❌ Negative & ❓ Questions

I/You/He/She/We/They + didn't + base verb
With did, the verb does not change!
✗ I didn't went to the party.
✓ I didn't go to the party.
Did + subject + base verb? → Did you kiss him? · Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
5 · PRONUNCIATION CHALLENGE

How does the past sound?

Touch your throat lightly and say the words. The -ed ending has 3 sounds:

/t/

Voiceless – throat quiet
baked, cooked, helped, kissed

/d/

Voiced – throat vibrates
played, cleaned, loved, moved

/id/

Voiced – extra syllable
started, wanted, decided, needed

Your turn: 🔊 listen, then 🎤 say it. (Mic check works in Chrome/Safari. If it doesn't, use the manual check.)
