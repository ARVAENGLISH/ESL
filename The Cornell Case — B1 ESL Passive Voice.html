PASSIVE VOICE ⏰

THE GOLDEN RULE
BE + V3

In the Passive Voice, BE carries the tense. The main verb stays in V3 / past participle.

Move the receiver to the front

FOUR TENSES

TIMELINE

Click each point on the timeline.

WHEN DO WE USE ACTIVE VOICE?

Use Active Voice when the person or organization performing the action is important.

EXAMPLECornell opened the investigation.

Focus: Cornell

WHEN DO WE USE PASSIVE VOICE?

Use Passive Voice when:

  • the action is more important;
  • the result is more important;
  • we don't know who performed the action;
  • the person is obvious;
  • the style is formal or objective.
EXAMPLES The case was reported.
Several interviews were conducted.
The investigation was reopened.
Potential charges are being considered.

THE USE OF BY

Use BY + person/organization when you want to identify who performed the action.

WITH BYThe case was investigated by Cornell University Police.

But BY is optional when the person is not important or obvious.

WITHOUT BYThe case was reopened.

Do not add BY to every passive sentence.
