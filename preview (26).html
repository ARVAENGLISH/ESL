04 / LOOK

PROJECT STATUS MEETING

Laura: Hi, Camila. Do you have a minute? I want to check the status of the project.
Camila: Sure. Overall, we're making good progress. We have completed the excavation and the foundation is in progress.
Laura: Great. What about the building permit?
Camila: That's the main problem right now. We submitted the documents two weeks ago, but we haven't received the final approval yet.
Laura: I see. So, is the project behind schedule?
Camila: A little. We have lost about five days because of the permit. The construction team stopped some activities last week.
Laura: What activities are still pending?
Camila: The structural work is in progress, but the next phase is pending. We can't start it until we have the license.
Laura: Have you talked to the person in charge of the permit?
Camila: Yes, I spoke with her yesterday. She said they have reviewed the documents, but they need one additional document from the municipality.
Laura: Okay. Have we sent that document?
Camila: Yes, we sent it this morning. So now we're waiting for their response.
Laura: That's good. What's the new deadline?
Camila: We have moved the deadline by one week. If we receive the license this week, we can continue with the next activity on Monday.
Laura: So, are we still on schedule?
Camila: Not exactly. We're currently behind schedule, but we have a plan to recover the time.
Laura: Perfect. Let's keep monitoring the permit and update the schedule when we receive the approval.
Camila: Absolutely. I'll send you an update this afternoon.

07 / GRAMMAR

Simple Past vs Present Perfect

SIMPLE PAST

Acción terminada en el pasado, normalmente sabemos cuándo ocurrió.

🙋🏻‍♀️ Subject + past verb + complement
🙋🏻‍♀️ We submitted the documents two weeks ago.
🙋🏻‍♀️ She spoke with the contractor yesterday.
🙋🏻‍♀️ They sent the document this morning.

PRESENT PERFECT

Acción ocurrida en el pasado pero conectada con el presente. El momento exacto no es importante o no se menciona.

🙋🏻‍♀️ Subject + have/has + V3 + complement
🙋🏻‍♀️ We have completed the excavation.
🙋🏻‍♀️ They have reviewed the documents.
🙋🏻‍♀️ She has sent the report.
KEY DIFFERENCE

Simple Past → ¿Cuándo ocurrió?
We submitted the documents two weeks ago.

Present Perfect → ¿Cuál es el resultado / situación actual?
We have submitted the documents.
