📖 LOOK THE STORY

Why did I break up with my ex?

By Kat ❤️

That's water under the bridge.

I met my ex-boyfriend John when we were in college. We were in the same career, the same semester, and the same classes.

It all started as friendship, but honestly, I found him attractive from the first moment we met.

For one year, we were just friends. In our third semester, we had a little fling: flirting, holding hands, and kissing at parties. Nothing serious.

Later, we both got partners, but our friendship didn't stop. We graduated, and I moved to France for an exchange.

When I came back home, I got in touch with my college friends. That's when I met John again.

Nothing romantic. We were just two old friends.

Then I decided to move alone. I asked John to help me, and that's when our relationship started.

We dated and spent nights at my place, but we weren't official.

After five months, he asked me to be his girlfriend.

Four years passed.

I traveled alone because he didn't want to join my adventures. I waited two and a half years to meet his parents.

It was okay... until it wasn't.

Little by little, I got tired of asking him to have initiative, make plans, and take me on proper dates — not just Netflix and pizza at home.

I really thought we were going to be together forever.

I pictured a family with him, a house, and two dogs called Tinto and Panela.

In December 2024, he promised that by January 2025 we would move in together.

It didn't happen.

I went on another solo trip.

When I came back from my trip, he promised again he would move in.

But on October 12, 2025, two days before my birthday, we had a long conversation.

I asked, “Are you actually moving with me this year?”

He was silent.

I understood.

Living with me wasn't in his plans.

We broke up that night.

I cried a river.

It has been almost a year. I would like to say it's all water under the bridge, but sometimes I still cry when I remember all the things I wanted to do with him that will never happen.

🔎 COMPREHENSION ANSWER FIRST, THEN CHECK

🔍 DISCOVERY

Look at the story.

Which words describe a state or situation?

Which words describe a finished action?

What is the difference between them?

was/were = state, situation, feeling, place. Simple Past verbs = finished actions.

🧠 GRAMMAR

🔵 PAST VERB TO BE

WAS / WERE for: situations, descriptions, feelings, locations, identities, states.

+ I was tired. / They were in college.

− I wasn't happy. / They weren't official.

? Was he serious? / Were they together?

Did she was happy?

✅ Was she happy?

With was/were, we DON'T use DID.

🟠 PASADO PISADO — SIMPLE PAST

We use Simple Past for finished actions in the past.

Kat met John.

They graduated.

She moved to France.

They broke up.

⚠️ MANTRA DE CONJUGAR

EN EL PASADO PISADO NO HAY CONJUGACIONES.

Everyone gets: DID / DIDN'T + VERB
The subject DOES NOT change the verb after DID.

+
I did go.
She did go.
They did go.
−
I didn't go.
She didn't go.
They didn't go.
?
Did I go?
Did she go?
Did they go?

❌ Did she went? ✅ Did she go?
❌ She didn't went. ✅ She didn't go.

🧩 GRAMMAR FORMULA

POSITIVE +SUBJECT + PAST VERBKat met John.
NEGATIVE −SUBJECT + DIDN'T + BASE VERBKat didn't meet John.
QUESTION ?DID + SUBJECT + BASE VERB?Did Kat meet John?
⚠️ EXCEPTIONVerb To Be does NOT use DID.Was Kat happy?
Were they together?

🚦 TRAFFIC LIGHT ¿EN QUÉ COLOR ESTÁS HOY?

🟢 GREEN — LO TIENES

Si estás en GREEN, ya entiendes la diferencia.

WAS/WERE habla de cómo alguien/algo era, estaba, se sentía o dónde estaba.

SIMPLE PAST habla de una acción terminada.

👉 John was silent. → John estaba en silencio.

👉 Kat met John. → Kat conoció a John.

Estado/situación → WAS/WERE
Acción terminada → SIMPLE PAST

🟡 YELLOW — MANEJAS LA IDEA, PERO OJO

El error más común es usar DID + pasado.

Cuando aparece DID o DIDN'T, el verbo vuelve a su forma básica.

❌ Did Kat met John? ✅ Did Kat meet John?

❌ Kat didn't went. ✅ Kat didn't go.

DID ya está marcando el pasado. Por eso el segundo verbo NO necesita volver a marcar el pasado.

DID + BASE VERB
DIDN'T + BASE VERB
DID + SUBJECT + BASE VERB?

🔴 RED — NECESITO AYUDA

Si estás en RED, vuelve a estas tres preguntas:

1. ¿Estoy describiendo una situación, estado o ubicación? → WAS / WERE

2. ¿Estoy hablando de una acción terminada? → SIMPLE PAST

3. ¿Tengo DID o DIDN'T? → El verbo que sigue vuelve a su forma básica.

IDEASTRUCTUREEXAMPLE
Estadowas/wereShe was tired.
Acciónpast verbShe traveled.
Negativadidn't + baseShe didn't travel.
PreguntaDid + subject + base?Did she travel?
Pregunta BEWas/Were + subject?Was she tired?

🚨 THE PASADO PISADO RULE 🪦

DID already did the past.
So DON'T put the verb in the past again.

Did + go ✅

Did + went ❌

Didn't + break ✅

Didn't + broke ❌

BUT... WAS/WERE don't need DID.

Was she tired? ✅

Did she was tired? ❌
