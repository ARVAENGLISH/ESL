Look: Alcatraz Prison

Alcatraz is a small island in San Francisco, California. It is famous for its old prison.

The prison opened in 1934 for dangerous criminals. It was very difficult to escape because the island is surrounded by cold water.

Some prisoners tried to escape. In 1962, three prisoners escaped and were never found.

The prison closed in 1963. Today, Alcatraz is a museum and tourist attraction. Visitors can see the old cells and learn about its history.

🚨 TODAY'S CASE

This morning, two very dangerous prisoners escaped from prison.

The police believe they are somewhere in San Francisco.

But there's another problem...

🚨 A WOMAN IS MISSING.

The police need your help.

Can you identify the two fugitives?
Can you find the missing woman?

09

Discovery: How Can We Describe People?

Instead of just saying:

"That's Jennifer."

or:

"She's tall and blonde."

You can describe people with what you can see right now:

what they are doing

or

where they are hanging out.

Today we're going to learn two secret weapons.

10

Secret Weapon #1 — Action-Packed Clues

-ING VERBS

When someone is doing something right now, we can use:

VERB + ING

Examples (click each verb):

Spelling tip: most verbs just add -ing. If a verb ends in -e, remove it (make → making). Short verbs double the last letter (sit → sitting).

BEFORE ➜ AFTER: Click to see the clue

11

Secret Weapon #2 — Style & Location

When you want to describe someone's style, physical features, or exact location, use prepositions.

More examples

"She's the woman with long black hair."

"She's the tall one in jeans."

"They're the people next to the window."

"He's the man in the black jacket."

Quick check: choose the preposition

12

Combining Clues

ACTION (-ing)APPEARANCELOCATION

ACTION + APPEARANCE

-ing + clothes
She's the woman wearing a white shirt.

ACTION + LOCATION

-ing + place / person
He's the man talking to Brooke.

APPEARANCE + LOCATION

clothes + place
She's the woman in the red dress next to the door.

EVERYTHING TOGETHER

action + appearance + location + action
She's the woman wearing white jeans, sitting next to the ginger woman, and drinking a mojito.
