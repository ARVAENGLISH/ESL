Grammar in Context

Direct vs. Indirect Questions

In English, you can't ask two questions at the same time.
En inglés es imposible tener dos preguntas al mismo tiempo. Do you know…? / Can you tell me…? / Could you tell me…? ya son la pregunta. Lo que viene después se escribe como una afirmación (sujeto + verbo).
✗ Do you know where is the ATM?Dos preguntas pegadas: "Do you know…?" + "Where is…?"
✓ Do you know where the ATM is?Una sola pregunta. La segunda parte va en orden normal.

🧩 The formula

Do you know / Can you tell me / Could you tell me + question word
where, what, how… + subject + verb … ?

🤔 When do we use them?

Direct questions

  • Con amigos, familia, personas de confianza.
  • Situaciones informales y rápidas.
  • "Where's the bathroom?"

Indirect questions

  • Con desconocidos: en la calle, en una tienda, en un hotel.
  • Cuando quieres sonar más amable y educado.
  • Cuando no sabes si la otra persona conoce la respuesta.
  • "Excuse me, could you tell me where the bathroom is?"

🔧 How to build them

Wh-questionsIndirect questions
Wh-questions with beIndirect questions
Where is the nearest ATM?Could you tell me where the nearest ATM is?
Where are the restrooms?Do you know where the restrooms are?
Wh-questions with doIndirect questions
How often do the buses run?Can you tell me how often the buses run?
What time does the bookstore open?Do you know what time the bookstore opens?
Wh-questions with canIndirect questions
Where can I catch the bus?Do you know where I can catch the bus?

1. With BE

El verbo be pasa después del sujeto.

Where is the hotel? →
Do you know where the hotel is?

2. With DO / DOES / DID

Do/does/did desaparecen y el verbo principal se conjuga.

What time does it open? →
Do you know what time it opens?

Where did he go? →
Do you know where he went?

3. With CAN

Can pasa después del sujeto.

Where can I get a map? →
Do you know where I can get a map?

Scenario

Operation: Nightingale 🏥

CASE #0417

CASE FILE

11:30 p.m. A woman arrives at Saint Mary's Hospital with an unconscious man. The doctors say he was drugged.

On his arm there is a new tattoo: NIGHTINGALE. Detective Owens talks to everybody. Who is guilty?

👥 The 5 players

👮Detective OwensThe police
👩Rosa DíazThe woman who brought him
🛌Leo GrantThe patient
💍Clara GrantLeo's wife
💼Max RyderLeo's business partner

📜 How to play

1. Open only your fileAhí está tu información y tus preguntas.
2. Indirect questionsTodas las preguntas se hacen con Do you know…? / Could you tell me…? / Can you tell me…?
3. Talk to the detectiveEl detective entrevista a cada persona. Cada persona también le hace una pregunta al detective.
4. One person is lying 🤫El detective debe descubrir quién es el culpable.

🗺️ The investigation

Interview 1👮 Detective → 👩 Rosa
👮Detective asksWhere did you find him?
👮Detective asksWhat time did you find him?
👩Rosa asksWhat happened to him?
➡️ Rosa: "Look! He's waking up. Talk to him!"

👮Detective asksWhat is your name?
👮Detective asksWhere were you last night?
🛌Leo asksWhere am I?
➡️ Leo: "I don't remember… Call my wife, Clara!"
Interview 3👮 Detective → 💍 Clara

⚖️ Detective's decision

Detective, ¿quién es el culpable? Elige y explica por qué en inglés.

The culprit

Clara Grant, Leo's wife. She drugged Leo to get the password of his safe ($1,000,000). Leo tattooed the password on his arm: NIGHTINGALE.

The clues

  1. Clara said: "Leo had dinner with Max." But Max was in Medellín.
  2. Leo told Max: "Someone in my house wants my money."
  3. Rosa's receipt from the Blue Café says CLARA GRANT.
