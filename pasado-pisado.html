Grammar · Simple Past

Usamos el simple past para hablar de acciones que empezaron y terminaron en el pasado: yesterday, last weekend, last year, when I was a child…

Regular verbs

verb + -ed

work → workedlike → liked study → studiedstop → stopped stay → stayedvisit → visited

Irregular verbs

Cambian completamente 🤯

go → wenthave → had do → didmake → made take → tooksee → saw buy → boughteat → ate get → gotcome → came
Simple past
+I went to the beach. She worked on Saturday.
–I didn't go to the beach. She didn't work on Saturday.
?Did you go to the beach? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.
Wh-?Where did you go? What did you do? Who did you go with?
💡 Después de did / didn't el verbo vuelve a su forma base: Did you went go?
