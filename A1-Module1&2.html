Simple present
  • He/She/It: verb + -s (works, plays). Irregular: have → has, go → goes, do → does.
  • Wh- questions: does with he/she/it, do with I/you/we/they. Don’t add -s to the verb: Where does she live?
Time expressions
  • in the morning/afternoon/evening · at night · at clock times · on days.
