🟡 Vamos paso a paso

📘 ¿Cuándo usamos el presente simple?

Para hablar de rutinas, hábitos, hechos y cosas que pasan con frecuencia, y también para gustos.

I work every day.
She likes pizza.

¿Cuándo usamos DO?

Con I, you, we, they (y sujetos plurales: my parents, the students).

Do you work?

¿Cuándo usamos DOES?

Con he, she, it (y sujetos singulares: Maria, my dad, the dog).

Does she work?

🐍 ¿Por qué el verbo no lleva -s después de DOES?

Porque DOES ya lleva la S. Cuando aparece DOES (o DOESN'T), él hace el trabajo y el verbo principal vuelve a su forma original.

❌ Does she works?
✅ Does she work?

+Positive

Sujeto + verbo (con -s para he/she/it).

−Negative

Sujeto + don't / doesn't + verbo base.

I don't work.

1️⃣ ¿Cuándo usamos el presente simple?

Lo usamos para hablar de cosas que pasan con regularidad:

🔁 Rutinas: I wake up at 6.
🔂 Hábitos: She drinks coffee every morning.
🌎 Hechos: Cali is in Colombia.
📅 Cosas regulares: The bus comes at 7.
❤️ Gustos: He likes pizza. / I don't like cold weather.

+2️⃣ Oraciones positivas

I / YOU / WE / THEY → sujeto + verbo base

I work.
They live in Cali.

HE / SHE / IT → sujeto + verbo + S

She works.
He lives in Cali.

Algunos verbos son irregulares:

have → hasdo → doesgo → goes

Otros: watch → watches, study → studies.

−3️⃣ Oraciones negativas

I / YOU / WE / THEY → do not / don't + verbo base

I don't work.

HE / SHE / IT → does not / doesn't + verbo base

She doesn't work.
❌ She doesn't works.
✅ She doesn't work.

?4️⃣ Preguntas

DO + I / you / we / they + verbo base?

Do you work here?

DOES + he / she / it + verbo base?

Does she work here?
❌ Does she works here?
✅ Does she work here?
🐍 Si aparece DOES, la S se queda en la casa de DOES. El verbo vuelve a su forma original.
