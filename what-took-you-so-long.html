⚖️ The big difference — tap “Why?”

💡 Easy rule: If you say WHEN (last night, last year, in 2024) → Simple Past. If the time is not finished or not important (ever, already, yet, since, for) → Present Perfect.

⏰ Being late: North America vs. Colombia

In North America, people usually like others to be on time, but for some occasions it's OK to be a little late. For example, people should always arrive on time or a little early for a business appointment, work, or a class. However, when they meet a friend or someone invites them to dinner, it's OK to arrive 5 to 10 minutes late. For an informal party, it's OK to arrive 15 to 30 minutes late.

🇺🇸 vs 🇨🇴 How late is OK?
